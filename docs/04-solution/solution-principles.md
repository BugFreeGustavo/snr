## Purpose

This document defines the principles that guide solution design within SNR.

Solution Principles ensure that every capability, feature and future implementation remains aligned with the product's purpose.

Rather than prescribing implementation details, they provide a consistent framework for evaluating product decisions.

Whenever multiple solutions are possible, these principles should guide the decision-making process.

## Why Solution Principles Matter

The domain explains professional reality.

The architecture protects that reality.

The solution transforms that understanding into value for the Professional.

Without guiding principles, individual features may solve isolated problems while gradually weakening the overall product vision.

Solution Principles ensure that every evolution of SNR reinforces its central promise.

---

# Principle 01

## Decisions Before Information

Information is valuable only when it improves a future professional decision.

SNR does not preserve information for its own sake.

It preserves knowledge that helps the Professional make better decisions throughout future engagements.

---

# Principle 02

## Context Before Records

Individual records provide limited value when separated from their professional context.

The Professional should understand why information exists, how it was created and how it relates to previous work.

Context transforms information into reusable knowledge.

---

# Principle 03

## Preserve, Don't Decide

Only the Professional can determine what knowledge deserves to be preserved.

SNR provides a consistent environment for preserving professional knowledge without attempting to determine its importance.

---

# Principle 04

## Reduce Cognitive Effort

Every capability should reduce the mental effort required to perform professional work.

Professionals should spend less time searching for information they already possess and more time applying their expertise.

Reducing cognitive effort creates professional value.

---

# Principle 05

## Relationships Before Projects

Projects are temporary.

Professional relationships often continue for years.

The solution should preserve the continuity of those relationships so that every new engagement benefits from previous experience.

---

# Principle 06

## Knowledge Before Administration

Administrative information is essential to professional work.

Its greatest value, however, lies in the knowledge it preserves rather than the administrative process itself.

The solution should transform administrative effort into reusable professional knowledge.

---

# Principle 07

## Reuse Before Rediscovery

Knowledge that has already been acquired should remain available whenever similar situations arise.

The Professional should never have to rediscover knowledge that already exists.

---

# Principle 08

## Value Before Features

Every feature introduced into SNR should fulfil one or more documented capabilities.

Features exist to deliver professional value.

They should never exist simply because they are technically possible.

---

## Cross-Principle Observations

Together, these principles define how SNR delivers value.

They ensure that every capability contributes to preserving professional knowledge, reducing cognitive effort and improving future decision-making.

Although implementation will evolve over time, these principles should remain stable.

---

## Principles Before Features

Features evolve.

Interfaces evolve.

Technology evolves.

The principles guiding the solution should remain stable.

Every product decision should be explainable through one or more Solution Principles.

---

## Guiding Principle

Professional knowledge creates value only when it improves future decisions.

> The Professional should never have to rediscover knowledge that already exists.
