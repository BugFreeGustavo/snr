## Purpose

This section contains the strategic documentation that defines the identity of SNR.

It explains why the product exists, the problem it solves, who it is designed for and the principles that guide every product decision.

These documents should be read before exploring the business domain, software architecture or implementation details.

---

## Scope

The Product documentation is intentionally technology-independent.

Its purpose is to define the product itself rather than its implementation.

Topics covered in this section include:

- Product Philosophy
- Product Vision
- Product Principles
- Product Positioning

## Reading Order

For the best understanding of SNR, the recommended reading order is:

1. `../../MANIFESTO.md`

3. `principles.md`

## Design Philosophy

The Product documentation answers four fundamental questions.

| Question | Document |
|----------|----------|
| Why does SNR exist? | `../../MANIFESTO.md` |
| Where is SNR going? | `vision.md` |
| How are product decisions made? | `principles.md` |
| Why is SNR different? | `positioning.md` |

Understanding these four documents should provide sufficient context to understand every subsequent design and engineering decision within the project.
