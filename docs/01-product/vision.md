## Purpose

This document defines the long-term direction of SNR.

It describes what the product aspires to become, the problems it intends to solve and the principles that guide its evolution.

Unlike implementation plans or roadmaps, the Product Vision is intentionally long-lived and should remain stable as the product grows.

## Vision Statement

SNR aims to become the operational memory for independent professionals.

The product exists to ensure that valuable experience is preserved, organised and made available whenever it becomes useful again.

Rather than helping users remember what happened, SNR should help them benefit from everything they have already learned.

---

## The Future We Want to Create

We envision a future where freelancers no longer waste time reconstructing information from previous projects.

Instead of searching through emails, folders, spreadsheets and conversations, they begin every project with immediate access to the operational knowledge accumulated throughout their professional experience.

Every completed project strengthens the next one.

Knowledge compounds.

Experience becomes a competitive advantage.

SNR is designed to become the central operational workspace where that operational knowledge is preserved, connected and reused across projects.

Rather than replacing existing tools, it complements them by preserving the experience created while using them.

---

## Long-Term Objectives

The long-term objectives of SNR are to:

- Preserve operational experience across projects.
- Reduce administrative effort.
- Reduce cognitive load.
- Improve decision-making through historical knowledge.
- Increase the value of every completed project.
- Allow professionals to focus on delivering work instead of managing information.

These objectives should remain valid regardless of future technologies or implementation details.

---

## Strategic Direction

As SNR evolves, every significant product decision should strengthen one or more of the following strategic pillars.

### Preserve Experience

Operational knowledge should never be lost once a project has been completed.

The system should continuously transform project history into reusable knowledge.

---

### Reduce Friction

Administrative work should require as little effort as possible.

Whenever appropriate, repetitive work should be simplified through thoughtful product design and automation.

---

### Maintain Context

Information only becomes valuable when its context is preserved.

Relationships between clients, projects, deliverables, assets and credentials should remain visible throughout the product.

---

### Build Organisational Memory

SNR should progressively become a trusted source of operational knowledge.

The longer a user works with the product, the more valuable it should become.

---

## Success Criteria

The vision of SNR will be fulfilled when users naturally rely on the product before starting new work.

Success is not measured by the number of stored records.

Success is measured by how often previous experience helps users make better decisions.

---

## Vision Boundaries

To protect the identity of the product, SNR deliberately avoids expanding into unrelated domains.

The product is not intended to become:

- a generic task manager
- a CRM platform
- an accounting system
- an ERP solution
- a document storage platform

These problems are already well served by existing software.

SNR creates value by preserving operational experience, not by replacing specialised tools.

---

## Guiding Principle

Every completed project should make the next project easier.

If a proposed feature does not contribute towards that objective, its value should be carefully reconsidered.

---

## Closing Statement

The long-term ambition of SNR is simple.

To become the place where professional experience is preserved instead of forgotten.

Every project teaches something.

SNR exists to ensure those lessons continue creating value long after the work has been delivered.
