## Positioning Statement

SNR is an operational workspace designed for independent professionals.

Its purpose is to preserve operational experience across client projects, allowing freelancers to reuse knowledge instead of repeatedly reconstructing it.

Rather than competing as another productivity platform, SNR focuses on becoming the operational memory behind professional work.

---

## The Problem

Freelancers rarely lose information.

They lose context.

Client details exist in emails.

Project files exist in cloud storage.

Credentials exist in password managers.

Tasks exist in project management tools.

Notes exist in documents.

Invoices exist in accounting software.

Every tool stores part of the story.

None preserves the operational knowledge created while delivering the project.

---

## Our Perspective

Freelance work is not fundamentally a task management problem.

It is an operational knowledge problem.

Every completed project teaches lessons that should improve future work.

Most software records activity.

SNR preserves experience.

---

## Target Audience

SNR is designed for independent professionals who deliver work repeatedly for clients.

Typical users include:

- Software Developers
- Designers
- Audio Engineers
- Video Editors
- Photographers
- Consultants
- Architects
- Creative Professionals

Although these professions differ significantly, they share the same operational challenge:

Every completed project generates knowledge that should remain available for future work.

---

## What Makes SNR Different

Traditional productivity software focuses on organising work.

SNR focuses on preserving the knowledge created while performing that work.

Traditional software asks:

> What needs to be done?

SNR also asks:

> What should already be known before work begins?

This distinction defines the identity of the product.

---

## Competitive Position

SNR is designed to complement existing software rather than replace it.

| Category | Existing Tools | SNR's Role |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| Task Management | ClickUp, Trello, Asana | Preserve operational experience |
| Documentation | Notion, Confluence | Preserve contextual knowledge |
| CRM | HubSpot, Pipedrive | Preserve client operational history |
| Password Management | 1Password, Bitwarden | Preserve credential context |
| Cloud Storage | Google Drive, Dropbox | Preserve relationships between assets |

The value of SNR increases when operational knowledge can be connected across existing tools rather than duplicated inside them.

---

## What SNR Is Not

To preserve focus, SNR deliberately avoids becoming:

- a generic project management platform
- a CRM
- an ERP
- an accounting application
- a cloud storage platform
- a document management system

These categories are already served by mature products.

SNR creates value by solving a different problem.

---

## Product Promise

Every completed project should make the next project easier.

This promise should remain true regardless of future features, integrations or technologies.

Every product decision should strengthen this objective.

---

## Strategic Focus

SNR will always prioritise:

- Experience over information.
- Context over organisation.
- Knowledge over storage.
- Simplicity over feature count.
- Integration over duplication.
- Focus over expansion.

These priorities define the long-term identity of the product.

---

## Closing Statement

The purpose of SNR is not to manage more work.

Its purpose is to make future work easier through the preservation of operational experience.

Every completed project becomes an investment in the next one.

---

> **Experience should be preserved instead of recreated.**
