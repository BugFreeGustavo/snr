## Purpose

This document defines the official vocabulary of the SNR domain.

Each definition represents the canonical meaning of a business term.

Whenever terminology differs between implementation and documentation, the definitions contained in this glossary take precedence.

## Using This Glossary

Terms are intentionally concise.

Detailed behaviour, relationships and responsibilities are documented elsewhere.

The purpose of this document is to establish a consistent vocabulary across the entire product.

Terms are listed alphabetically.

---

# A

## Archived Project

A Project that is no longer expected to continue but whose historical knowledge remains available.

---

# B

## Business Event

A significant fact that has already occurred within the professional domain.

---

## Business Rule

A constraint or principle that governs professional work independently of software implementation.

---

# C

## Client

An individual or organisation that commissions professional work from the Professional.

---

## Credential

A reusable authentication resource owned or managed by the Professional.

Credentials provide authorised access to software, external services and client environments.

---

# D

## Deliverable

A business commitment that produces measurable value for a Client.

---

## Dependency

A condition that prevents work from progressing until it has been resolved.

---

## Domain

The professional reality that SNR models through software.

---

# H

## Historical Knowledge

Professional knowledge accumulated through Projects, Clients, Deliverables, Business Events and experience.

---

# K

## Knowledge

Experience that improves future professional decision-making.

---

# M

## Milestone

A significant business achievement reached during the lifecycle of a Project.

---

# P

## Planning

The activity of organising professional work before execution begins.

---

## Professional

The independent professional whose work, knowledge and resources are managed within SNR.

---

## Project

A professional engagement performed for a Client.

---

# R

## Relationship

A business association between two or more domain concepts.

---

## Revision

A requested modification made after a Deliverable has been reviewed.

---

# T

## Technical Asset

A reusable professional resource owned or managed by the Professional.

Technical Assets may support multiple Projects throughout their lifetime.

---

# U

## Ubiquitous Language

The shared business vocabulary used consistently throughout the SNR domain, documentation and implementation.

---

# W

## Workflow

A sequence of business activities performed to achieve a professional outcome.

---

## Terminology Principles

Every business term should have one canonical meaning.

Definitions should remain stable even when implementation changes.

New business terms should be defined in this Glossary before they are introduced elsewhere in the documentation.

---

## Guiding Principle

Shared language creates shared understanding.

Consistent terminology creates consistent software.
